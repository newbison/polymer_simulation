Key UI Decisions

Included

  • Stage badges update in real-time (active stage highlighted)
  • Ball-and-stick callout auto-appears for key events, fades after ~2s
  • Sliders are live — changing mid-simulation immediately affects rates
  • Mn and conversion give students quantitative insight

Deferred

  • Concentration vs. time chart (bottom bar toggle) — nice but adds complexity
  • Chain length distribution histogram — separate panel, quantum of value
  • Temperature/Arrhenius modeling — just use a "rate multiplier" slider for now
