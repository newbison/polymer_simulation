Reaction Rules (proximity-based)

Stage Particles Colliding Result Probability
Initiation Initiator molecule + (heat) → 2 primary radicals kd per timestep
Initiation Primary radical + monomer → growing chain (n=1) high (diffusion-limited)
Propagation Chain radical + nearby monomer → chain grows by +1 unit kp on proximity
Termination Two chain radicals → two dead chains (comb. or disprop.) kt on proximity

Particle Movement

Each particle gets a random Brownian displacement each frame. Chains move as connected segments — the radical end wiggles actively, the rest follows with some drag. This gives a natural "growing snake" look that students intuitively understand as chain growth.
